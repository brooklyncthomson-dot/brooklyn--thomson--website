On the ice — Brooklyn Thomson
The athlete

A story shaped by the ice.

Brooklyn began skating at age seven, inspired by her family's connection to hockey and encouraged by her mother to find her own place on the ice. Over time, figure skating became a meaningful passion and an important part of her life.

A member of the St. Margaret's Bay Skating Club, Brooklyn trains with coach Stephanie Steele. She has competed at several events and is continuing to develop as a singles skater, with a particular interest in jumps and jump combinations.

Training & development

Competitive in mindset.

Technical focus

Building jump technique and combinations while working toward greater consistency and control.

Competition experience

Experience at several skating events, with a focus on learning, performing, and developing through competition.

Training commitment

Currently working toward steady progress through regular skating sessions and continued skill development.

Resilience

Returning to the ice.

A fractured back interrupted Brooklyn's skating journey and required time away from the sport. Having recently returned to skating, she is entering a new phase focused on rebuilding, progressing at an appropriate pace, and continuing to pursue her ambitions.

“The goal is ambitious. The journey is ongoing. The work continues.”
Looking ahead

Big goals. One step at a time.

Brooklyn's long-term dream is to compete at the Olympic Games. Her focus now is on continuing to develop her skating, gaining experience, and working toward future competitive opportunities. Her story is still being written, one session and one milestone at a time.
